Walk into the gold JOB ring for missions. Buy guns at IRONSIDE ARMS, pick up taxi fares, hit the yellow stunt ramps, and hunt for 25 hidden cassettes across downtown, the suburbs, the docks and the beach. Gas pumps and red barrels explode, and Ironside Arms now sells rocket launchers and grenades. Everyone plays by the same rules: citizens, drivers and cops who commit crimes get the same wanted stars you do, and police chase, shoot and arrest them just like you. Take down wanted suspects for bounties. Lose the cops by staying out of sight, or pay $100 at the RESPRAY lot.
