GUNS: HOW TO

PC: grab a gun with the mouse (Drag tool), Q/E rotate it, R shoots.
Touch: grab it with your finger, use the ⟲ / ⟳ buttons at the bottom to rotate and the FIRE button at the top right to shoot.

Right-click drag grabs with any tool. Tab toggles this menu. Ctrl+Z or U undoes your last action.
