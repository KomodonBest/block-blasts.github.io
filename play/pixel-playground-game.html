WEAPONS: HOW TO

PC: grab a weapon with the mouse (Drag tool), Q/E rotate it, R uses it (shoot, rev the chainsaw, swing the katana, launch a rocket).
Touch: grab it with your finger, use the ⟲ / ⟳ buttons at the bottom to rotate and the big button at the top right to use it.

Right-click drag grabs with any tool. Tab toggles this menu. Ctrl+Z or U undoes your last action.
